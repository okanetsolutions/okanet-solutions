# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two distinct buyer worlds, both Spanish-speaking and Latin-America–centered (Venezuela first):

- **Content creators / influencers / personal brands** — evaluating OkaCRM. They want to professionalize their brand and collaborations without losing the voice that built their audience. They arrive from social channels, often on mobile, and judge credibility in seconds.
- **Internet service providers (ISPs) / regional fiber operators** — evaluating OkaISP, an AI-driven ERP. More technical, operations-minded buyers who care about cost reduction, automation, uptime, and accounting adapted to Venezuela. They want proof the AI is real, not marketing.

Context: visitors are deciding whether a Venezuelan software company is serious, capable, and durable enough to trust with a vertical platform. The site's job is to earn that trust and route them to the right product + WhatsApp contact.

## Product Purpose

Okanet Solutions C.A. is a Venezuelan software company (Caracas, since 2019) building **vertical** platforms for specific industries — not generic software. Two products anchor the brand: **OkaCRM** (CRM for content creators) and **OkaISP** (AI-powered ERP for internet providers). The marketing site exists to establish the company as credible, established, and craft-driven, differentiate the two products clearly, and convert interest into direct contact. Success = a serious-feeling first impression that makes both creator and ISP buyers reach out.

## Brand Personality

Three words: **artisanal, confident, technical-but-warm.** The voice is "software hecho a mano, a escala" — handmade craft delivered at scale. Editorial and considered ("construida con criterio"), never hype-y. It speaks Spanish naturally, leans on craft and provenance (Caracas coordinates, founding year in Roman numerals, RIF), and treats AI as a working engine ("inteligencia no decorativa"), not a marketing sticker. Two tonal registers coexist on purpose: OkaCRM reads warm-editorial (terracotta, serif display), OkaISP reads technical-structural (espresso/ink, mono type, dashboard mockups). Emotional goal: quiet confidence and trust — the feeling of an established studio that knows its craft.

## Anti-references

- **Cold corporate enterprise.** No sterile blue-and-gray B2B stiffness, no IBM/Oracle/SAP enterprise sterility. Okanet is warm, human, and craft-forward even when technical. Warmth and provenance carry the credibility, not corporate polish.
- Corollary tells to avoid: generic stock-photo professionalism, faceless "enterprise-grade" boilerplate, and any treatment that strips the Venezuelan/editorial character to look like a global SaaS template.

## Design Principles

- **Vertical, not generic.** Every design choice should feel specific to creators or to ISPs — the opposite of one-size-fits-all software. Two products, two worlds, two visual registers under one roof.
- **Show the craft.** Provenance and detail signal seriousness: coordinates, founding year, RIF, real-looking product mockups. Concrete specifics over vague claims.
- **AI as engine, not ornament.** Demonstrate intelligence through believable product surfaces and outcomes, never through buzzwords or decorative "AI" badges.
- **Warm credibility.** Trust comes from editorial restraint and human warmth, not corporate coldness. When in doubt, warmer and more considered, never more sterile.
- **Earn attention with hierarchy.** Editorial layout, deliberate spacing rhythm, and strong typographic contrast do the work — not loud effects.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal WCAG target committed. Best-effort: maintain readable contrast for body text against the warm-neutral backgrounds (the espresso/umber ink on bone/paper is the safe pairing; watch greige/stone on light surfaces for small text), and preserve the existing `prefers-reduced-motion` support already in the stylesheet. Spanish-first content; keep copy and labels in natural Latin American Spanish.
